Data Definitions

Definitions, filters and date ranges for every figure on this site.

Source and date range

All figures come from the built-in reports in Intercom, the customer support software BrightMove uses for its support chat and email. They cover Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026 (New York time). Intercom's report history begins October 2025. All screenshots were captured on Oct 27, 2026.

Terms used in Intercom's screens: "Fin AI Agent" is Intercom's name for the AI agent product that BrightMove runs as Wiz, so any Intercom label that says "Fin" refers to Wiz. "Teammate" means a person on BrightMove's support team.

Customer experience score (labeled "CX Score" in Intercom)

A score from 1 to 5 that Intercom's AI gives a closed customer conversation by reading it. The customer does not have to do anything. Scores of 4 and 5 count as positive. Each percentage is the share of scored conversations that received a 4 or 5.

When a conversation gets an experience score

The score is a feature of Intercom's AI. A conversation is scored only when all of these are true:

  • It is closed.
  • It happened over chat or email, not phone.
  • The customer started it.
  • It has at least 2 customer replies and 2 replies from a person or the AI agent.
  • It has enough clear information for the AI to evaluate.
  • It isn't spam.

The AI then weighs signals such as answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback to assign a score from 1 to 5. Only 3,272 of the 4,840 counted conversations met these conditions and have a score.

The experience scores used in this entry

Definitions are adapted from Intercom's report tiles. In each case, the AI rates each conversation on answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback.

  • Wiz only (Wiz-only CX): how well Wiz is working for customers, in conversations handled only by Wiz.
  • Wiz and a person together (Wiz + teammate CX): how well Wiz and the support team work together, in conversations handled by both.

Naming note: In Intercom's screens these appear as "Fin AI Agent CX Score" (Wiz only) and "Fin AI Agent and Teammate CX Score" (Wiz and a person together).

Customer satisfaction rating

An optional 1 to 5 rating the customer can give at the end of a chat (labeled "CSAT" in Intercom). The weekly review of Wiz conversations includes every low rating.

Wiz (AI agent) measures

Wiz figures cover Wiz conversations from Oct 1, 2025 to Sep 30, 2026 (New York time), under the same filter as every other figure, so they reflect real product-support questions.

  • Resolution rate (82.4%): Intercom's definition. A conversation counts as resolved when the customer confirms Wiz's answer helped ("Confirmed resolved"), or leaves without asking for more help after Wiz's answer ("Assumed resolved").
  • Deflection rate (68.7%): Intercom's definition. The share of conversations Wiz took part in that did not need to go to a person. A conversation counts as deflected when Wiz resolved it, or when the customer left before Wiz answered or after reacting negatively to Wiz's answer. Because it also counts customers who simply left, deflection is a measure of how much Wiz handled without a handover, not of how many questions it solved; resolution rate is the measure of that. Source: Intercom's reporting definitions for its AI agent.

The filter used for every figure

Every figure on this site is based on 4,840 Intercom conversations from Oct 1, 2025 to Sep 30, 2026 (New York time), after removing conversations that are not customer support.

How the 4,840 counted conversations are reached
Conversations
All conversations in the period5,524
Removed: tagged SPAM−345
Removed: tagged Testing−44
Removed: tagged account maintenance (Intercom tag "Non-LVE Related CX")−295
Counted in every figure4,840

299 conversations carry the account maintenance tag; 4 of them are also tagged SPAM or Testing and are counted once, under SPAM or Testing.

  • Why SPAM and Testing are removed: they are not real customer conversations.
  • Why account maintenance is removed: these requests (such as adding users and changing settings) are routine admin tasks, not customer-experience conversations. They are reviewed separately each week to decide which tasks to turn into features customers can do themselves.
  • What the tag name means: LVE stands for LightHub Veracity Enhancement, a conversation showing that something in LightHub, BrightMove's help center, or in Wiz, its AI agent, needs refining. "Non-LVE Related CX" marks conversations that need no such refinement, such as account maintenance requests. The team began tagging account maintenance by hand in late October 2025, so a small number of October 2025 conversations may be included.

About the screenshots

  • All screenshots were captured on Oct 27, 2026 from Intercom's reports, using the date range and filter above.
  • The small green/red arrows next to each report tile are Intercom's comparison with the previous period. Intercom offers no way to hide them, and report history begins October 2025, so they are not a meaningful comparison and are not cited on this site.
  • The sample-size screenshots are cropped to the filter bar and item count; no conversation rows are shown. Intercom cuts off the filter label at the right edge, so the full filter is written out in the section above: Excludes conversations tagged Conversation: SPAM, Conversation: Testing, or Conversation: Non-LVE Related CX (account maintenance).
  • On the Wiz (Fin AI Agent) performance screenshot, the small green arrows are the same previous-period comparison and are not cited.

Why figures move

Intercom's reports update over time: conversations reopen and ratings arrive late. The screenshots captured on Oct 27, 2026 are the source of truth for every figure on this site.
