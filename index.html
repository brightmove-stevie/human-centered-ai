BrightMove: evidence for the 2027 Stevie Awards entry

BrightMove makes cloud-based hiring software, known as an applicant tracking system (ATS), that helps recruiters find, track and hire candidates. Its customers are staffing agencies, in-house HR teams, and firms that run HR or recruiting for other companies. Because BrightMove is a standalone system, its customers' success depends on how well it connects with the other tools they use, and on the support behind it. In late 2025, BrightMove earned Platinum partner status in the Indeed partner program.

Human-Centered, AI-Powered

Human-Centered AI Innovation of the Year, Category 227. AI that strengthens human support, never replaces it: an AI support agent that answers customers at any hour, with people taking over whatever it cannot solve and checking its work every week.

The support model in one view

Customers reach BrightMove support through a chat window inside the software, or by email. Questions are handled in up to three steps:

3. Fixes the softwareEngineeringSoftware bugs, and problems connecting BrightMove with other tools—Under 24 hours

LightHub, the help center: LightHub is BrightMove's online help center, a library of how-to articles customers can search on their own. Wiz answers customers using these articles. The articles are organized around a recruiter's three main jobs: finding candidates (Source), contacting them (Engage) and tracking them through hiring (Track).

Intercom: BrightMove runs its support chat, email, AI agent and reporting on Intercom, a customer support software product. The screenshots on this site come from Intercom. Inside Intercom's screens, Wiz appears under Intercom's product name for its AI agent, "Fin AI Agent", and the support team appears as "Tier 1 Support".
